A. Utilitarian / Technical

No decoration. Flat dividers instead of cards. Tight spacing. Proportional sans-serif font. Solid backgrounds — no glass, grain, or glows. The tool disappears, the settings stay.

Think: GNOME Settings + well-designed terminal app

B. Clean & Refined

Minimal decoration, but still warm and polished. Subtle card groups with very light borders. Proportional font for UI, monospace only for values. Gentle surface elevation — no glass/grain/glows.

Think: Apple Settings + COSMIC Settings
